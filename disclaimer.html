05 Cost & payback estimates

The Cost & Payback calculator estimates what a master's or PhD is likely to cost, end to end, and how long it could take to earn that back. It is a planning model, not a forecast or a promise:

  • Graduate earnings figures are drawn from published data on domestic New Zealand graduates. As an international graduate you hold a time-limited post-study work visa and no guarantee of residence, so these figures are an upper reference point, not a prediction of what you personally will earn.
  • Part-time work income during study is estimated at the adult minimum wage as a floor, not an average, and — importantly — this income does not count toward Immigration New Zealand's visa funds requirement, even though it may reduce what your family actually needs to spend.
  • Payback is measured against the 3-year post-study work visa, not an open-ended career, and assumes no gaps in employment.
  • "Cheaper route" comparisons show other providers teaching a similar qualification at a lower published fee. They are informational, not a recommendation — check admission requirements, teaching quality, and your own priorities before switching providers on the strength of a fee difference alone.
  • The printable family decision sheet carries the same estimates and the same limitations; a figure printed on it is no more authoritative than the same figure on screen.

06 Provider-quality data

Where the Cost & Payback tool shows a provider's quality record, that record is the New Zealand Qualifications Authority's (NZQA) own published information — category, confidence statements, evaluation-report links, and Code of Practice signatory status — reproduced as-is. PathFinder does not score, rank, or editorialise it.

NZQA's External Evaluation and Review (EER) system ended on 19 January 2026, replaced by the Quality Assurance of Tertiary Education Providers Rules 2026. Any provider category shown is therefore historical, dated to when it was assigned, and displayed with a note that the system that produced it no longer operates. Universities carry no NZQA category by design — they are audited separately by the Academic Quality Agency and CUAP — and that absence is not a quality signal either way. PathFinder shows no Google, Glassdoor, or other third-party review scores.
