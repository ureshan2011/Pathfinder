The money works differently for each

PhD

Domestic fees. A stipend.

International PhD candidates pay the domestic tuition rate — about NZ$8,500 a year. Doctoral scholarships add NZ$28–33k on top, and most are awarded with admission rather than by separate application. You apply to a supervisor, not to an intake.

Master’s

Full international fees. No stipend.

Budget NZ$32,000–48,000 a year, plus the living costs the visa requires you to evidence. Scholarships here are competitive and deadline-bound, and usually discount fees rather than pay you. What follows the qualification is an immigration question — INZ publishes the post-study settings, and they change.
